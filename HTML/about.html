About Us

At Advanced Computers, we are passionate about helping New Zealand businesses and individuals succeed through reliable technology solutions. Based in New Zealand, we provide expert computer services, IT support, hardware solutions, and technology consulting designed to meet the needs of today's fast-changing digital world.

Since our founding, our mission has been simple: deliver innovative technology, exceptional service, and dependable support that our customers can trust.

Advanced Computers offers a comprehensive range of technology services, including:

  • Business IT solutions

Whether you're a home user looking for technical assistance or a business seeking a complete IT partner, our experienced team is ready to help.
